Privacy policy

This page explains what we do with the details you send us through the forms on this site.

Last updated: September 2026

Data controller

EV Pickup AS, org.nr 936 223 478, Bjorøyvegen 332, 5177 Bjorøyhamn, Norway. E-mail: post@elbilnorden.no.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: name, company, e-mail, phone, the van model or region you name, your message, and which language version you used.

Why

To answer your enquiry, arrange a test drive, and send you an offer or dealer terms. We do not use your details for anything else and we do not sell them.

Legal basis

Steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an agreement are deleted after six months. Where an agreement is made, accounting rules decide the retention period.

Who else sees it

Our web host processes the message on our behalf as a data processor. CIE AS builds and operates the site and also acts as processor for the form data. No one else receives your details.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics for anonymous visitor statistics, but only if you accept it in the cookie banner; you can decline, and you can change your choice by clearing the site's data in your browser. Google Analytics is provided by Google Ireland Ltd; IP addresses are anonymised. Your language choice is stored locally in your browser and never sent to us.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, and object to our processing. Write to post@elbilnorden.no. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
