About us

Vehicle and conversion from one place

We import Hudson electric commercial vehicles and the Nordic Camper modules to the Nordic market. One point of contact for the vehicle, the conversion and the paperwork.

We sell to distribution and service companies, tradespeople, municipalities, fleets — and private buyers who want a camper without giving up a usable van. EV Pickup AS, Bjorøy near Bergen, Norway.
